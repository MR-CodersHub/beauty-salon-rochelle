We hold the highest hygiene benchmarks in the beauty industry. Your wellness and safety are never compromised.

Our specialists clean, vacuum clippings/residues, and dispose of all materials in bio-friendly waste bags.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything You Need To Know

Clear answers to your doorstep beauty questions.

What do I need to provide for the beautician?

Nothing except a normal power outlet, comfortable seating, and access to a washbasin/water if booking hair washing or facial treatments. Our artists bring all protective floor sheets, professional lighting, towels, sanitized tools, and disposable waste bags.

How does the live beautician arrival tracking work?

Once your appointment is confirmed, you can open your live tracker in our portal. You will see your assigned therapist's name, photo, certifications, vehicle model, and real-time GPS location on the map with estimated arrival countdown.

Are there any travel charges or doorstep convenience fees?

No! LUXE has zero hidden fees. Travel, single-use kit costs, and post-service cleanup are 100% included in the displayed service prices for all locations within our service zones.

How do you guarantee product safety and hygiene?

We use 100% single-use sealed monodose sachets for skincare and haircare, opened in front of you. Metal tools are autoclaved and UV sterilized in sealed indicator pouches, and all linen/gowns are brand-new disposables.
