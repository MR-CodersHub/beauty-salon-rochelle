GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked

What do I need to prepare before the beautician arrives?

Nothing except a normal electrical power outlet, comfortable seating, and access to a washbasin/water if booking hair or facial treatments. We bring all protective floor draping, portable lighting, and sterile single-use kits.

How does live beautician arrival tracking work?

Once your booking is confirmed, open your User Dashboard or click Track Stylist in the navbar. You will see a live GPS map with your assigned specialist’s photo, rating, vehicle license plate, and real-time ETA countdown.

Are there any travel charges or doorstep convenience fees?

Zero! All listed prices include therapist travel, disposable single-use kit costs, and post-service sanitation. What you see is exactly what you pay.
