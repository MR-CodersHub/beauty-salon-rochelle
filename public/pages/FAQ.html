Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our doorstep beauty services, arrival tracking, and hygiene benchmarks.

Doorstep Preparation & Setup

What do I need to provide for the beautician?

Nothing except an ordinary power outlet, comfortable seating, and access to a washbasin/water if booking hair washing or facial treatments. We bring all protective floor drop-cloths, studio lighting, towels, sanitized tools, and disposal bags.

What if I have pets or small children at home?

We are 100% pet and family friendly! Our therapists use cord safety covers, whisper-quiet equipment, and non-toxic formulas. You can check the pet toggle when booking so the therapist arrives prepared.

Hygiene & Product Safety

How are metal tools sterilized?

All stainless steel shears, cuticle pushers, and tweezers are pre-sterilized at 120°C in medical autoclaves and sealed inside surgical blue indicator pouches that are opened in front of you.

Are the therapists background checked?

Yes. 100% of our therapists undergo rigorous police criminal background verification, government ID validation, and 500+ hours of cosmetology accreditation.

Live Arrival Tracking & Booking

How does live beautician tracking work?

Once confirmed, open your User Dashboard to view your assigned therapist's name, photo, ratings, vehicle model, and real-time GPS map trajectory with countdown ETA.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule or cancel directly from your User Dashboard up to 2 hours before the scheduled time with zero cancellation fee.
