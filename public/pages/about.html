THE ROCHELLE STANDARD

Why We Pioneer 100% Sealed Monodose Beauty

When our founder worked across luxury European fashion ateliers, she noticed that even the most prestigious brick-and-mortar salons used multi-dip product jars and generic sanitizing jars that failed hospital-grade benchmarks.

We eliminated the compromises. Every single formulation is sealed in individual monodose sachets opened in your presence. All steel tools undergo 120°C autoclave heat sterilization in surgical blue pouches, and our therapists bring protective floor drop cloths to guarantee a spotless finish.
