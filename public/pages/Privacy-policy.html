TRANSPARENCY & DATA PRIVACY

Privacy Policy & Confidentiality

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 • Version 4.2

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-precision doorstep beauty transformations, ROCHELLE collects information you provide directly when creating an account or booking treatments. This includes your name, phone number, email address, physical doorstep address, gate codes, parking instructions, and skin/hair allergy profiles.

2. How We Protect Your Doorstep & Entry Details

Your residential address, gate codes, and doorbell instructions are strictly encrypted with 256-bit AES encryption. These details are only revealed to your assigned, background-checked master therapist 30 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment and are automatically hidden upon appointment completion.

3. Skin Diagnostic & Allergy Records

Health notes regarding allergies (e.g. nut oils, latex, sulfate sensitivities) are stored securely in compliance with beauty wellness standards to ensure customized single-use monodose kits are curated safely for every visit.

4. Non-Disclosure & Zero Data Selling

ROCHELLE never sells, rents, or monetizes client personal information to third-party advertisers. Data is exclusively utilized to route verified specialists and process seamless cashless transactions.

5. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You maintain the right to view, modify, or permanently delete your booking history and stored addresses at any time directly through your User Dashboard or by contacting our Data Privacy Officer at privacy@rochelleathome.com.
